# Contributing to the Antares Engine ecosystem

This repository uses `LICENSE.md` (ASE-ADG-LIC-2026-10-16-1.0) and `PJV.md` (ASE-ADG-PJV-2026-10-16-1.0). Read both before submitting work. This file explains their workflow and does not add another license.

## Public use

Public Components may be downloaded, cloned, forked and developed without a PaaS subscription, including after cancellation. Follow the LIC for contribution duties, redistribution and commercial Products. An independent game, client or tool remains your Product; an Engine module or plugin developed for it belongs in the contribution process.

## Before development

1. Identify whether you act personally or for an entity and obtain the necessary authority.
2. Propose the extension's purpose, API relationships and boundary from your Product to the designated project authority. Use a private authorized channel for confidential work.
3. Obtain the recorded scope approval, canonical repository assignment and access. Record acceptance of the two versioned documents through the onboarding process.
4. Initialize the assigned repository within the prescribed superrepository/subrepository structure. Do not wait for a finished extension before making development visible.

## During development

- Commit and push development states continuously within the working process.
- Follow the actual repository's coding, interface, version, build and documentation standards.
- Include the README, integration information and required third-party notices.
- Keep company forks synchronized with the assigned project repository; a private final delivery is not a substitute.
- Do not commit secrets, private customer data or materials you cannot license.
- Use review and pull requests as instructed. Review does not defer the contribution grant until merge.

## Contribution acknowledgment

The onboarding acceptance record must establish the following acknowledgment; copying this text into every commit is not required:

> I have received and accept ASE-ADG-LIC-2026-10-16-1.0 and ASE-ADG-PJV-2026-10-16-1.0 for my identified participation. I am authorized to act in the stated personal or company capacity and to grant the rights in the contributions I submit. I understand that this includes initial and intermediate development states, continuing contribution rights after departure, and the stated rights for Jan Ohlmann, the Licensor and authorized ADG members. I have disclosed applicable third-party conditions and will not submit incompatible material or secrets without authorization.

An acknowledgment cannot cure missing authority. Resolve it before submission. Do not put private identity records or company revenue in public issues.

## Commercial Products

Report external alpha, beta, early access and launch under LIC section 9. Accept the commercial provisions before monetization. The lifetime Product allowance is USD 100,000; the rate above it is 5%, with no payment for quarters below USD 1,000 Product Revenue. Reporting/payment is due within 45 days after quarter end. PaaS purchases backend service separately.
