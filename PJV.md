# Antares Developer Group Project Participation Agreement

**Identifier: ASE-ADG-PJV-2026-10-16-1.0**  
**Issue date: 16 October 2026**

## Preamble

This Project Participation Agreement governs participation in the Antares Developer Group (ADG) and the handling of submitted contributions. Its paramount and declared aim is fidelity to the conceptual work of Jan Ohlmann as founder and concept author (section 1.3) and the realization of the philosophical guiding principle underlying that conceptual work.

The philosophical guiding principle is: **Complexity is a feature.**

It is the core of the entire Antares universe. It applies to the whole work: to the Antares Simulation Engine, to every game and application created with it within the project, such as Antares Open World or Antares Vivarium, and to all approved ecosystem work (section 1.2).

We see Antares as a sandbox also in the sense of “playing in the game with complexity”. We love complexity and metagaming. It is one of the founding philosophies of Antares to support the player who loves complexity instead of aligning the work with the player who seeks simplicity alone. There are enough games that have lost complexity through generalization over time and have therefore become uninteresting to the makers and founders of Antares.

Antares therefore expressly opposes commerce-driven reduction of content. The depth, intricacy and content of the work are not simplified, generalized or removed merely to make a product more broadly marketable. Commercial exploitation under the LIC and the customer terms remains unaffected; it serves the work and does not determine its content.

Join the project if you love immersing yourself in complexity, if new ideas and different paths do not deter you, and if you want to grasp the quintessence of the optimum through interaction and complexity as close to reality as possible.

With Antares we want to establish a new form of game culture: a living player culture carried by active metagaming, with a far-reaching and dynamic life of its own.

Only a person who shares this guiding principle and aligns their participation with it becomes an ADG Member; the Member confirms this by accepting this PJV (section 2.3). A person who does not share it does not become an ADG Member. The guiding principle is hereby disclosed in advance as part of the communicated project ethics. It is the yardstick for admission (section 2), for conceptual approval by Jan (sections 1.3 and 3.1) and for the assessment of proposals and contributions (section 4.3; LIC section 6.1).

The guiding principle concerns the substantive direction of participation in the work. It does not authorize investigation of a Member's private life or personal characteristics beyond that confirmation (section 2.1) and creates no additional work, availability or payment duty (section 6.2).

Public use under the LIC does not require membership (section 1.4). Independent Products (LIC section 2.3) and the contribution and approval duties under LIC section 6 remain unaffected. The LIC prevails for license scope, Product boundaries, royalties and contribution survival (section 10.2).

## 2. Admission and evidence

2.1 Admission requires the identity of the Member, a private contact, an identified project role and any represented entity. Only information necessary for identification, authority, access and lawful administration is collected. No general authority to investigate a Member's private life is granted.

2.2 An entity identifies the person authorized to accept and the personnel allowed to act for it. Personnel act within that authorization. A separate personal contribution role must be distinguished from company work. Employment or contractor permission requirements cannot be bypassed by a personal Git account.

2.3 Before access or first contribution, the Coordinator makes the LIC and PJV available, records their identifiers and the Member's acceptance, and confirms the approved work and access. The initialization and first authorized push may serve as acceptance evidence after disclosure, where legally effective. Subsequent commits under the accepted arrangement need no new paper agreement.

2.4 The membership record identifies the Member, represented entity if any, relevant accounts, role, applicable document versions, acceptance evidence, approved work and access changes. It is not a public directory of private addresses, identity documents or credentials. Git authorship and attribution records are kept separately from sensitive onboarding information.

## 3. Project authority

3.1 Jan approves the simulation purpose, new module/plugin scope and the persons delegated to make technical decisions. A maintainer's repository permission is not by itself authority to change licenses, waive royalties or promise company services.

3.2 An advisory group may support technical planning and dispute resolution. Appointment or delegation is recorded. No former committee member is assumed to retain a role merely because named in a historical agreement. Delegates may act only within their recorded authority.

3.3 Founder-level technical responsibilities remain independent of share ownership in the Coordinator. A change of corporate management does not automatically authorize that manager to relicense Jan's privately held materials. Conversely, this PJV does not override lawful corporate administration.

3.4 If Jan cannot perform a technical approval function, previously documented delegates may continue within their authority. Decisions reserved to the private rightsholder require that rightsholder or a lawfully authorized representative. This Agreement does not invent a private succession or estate arrangement.

## 4. Scope approval and repository structure

4.1 A proposal for a new module or plugin must identify its simulation purpose, functional boundary, relevant APIs, intended repository and expected public or restricted access. The proposal must distinguish the extension from the Member's own Product. The landscape-editor/erosion-module distinction in the LIC applies.

4.2 Approval is recorded before development of the extension begins. The designated repository is initialized within the project's prescribed superrepository/subrepository structure. Appropriate individual or service credentials are issued through the authorized access process.

4.3 Project approval is based on simulation relevance, compatibility with published architecture and documentation, lawful content and communicated project ethics. It is not an assurance that all proposed ideas will be accepted. Decisions should identify the practical reason and any necessary adjustment rather than invoke previously undisclosed restrictions retroactively.

4.4 Existing module fixes and tasks use the applicable task and review workflow. A material scope change must be raised before work is redirected to the new scope. A technical approval does not waive obligations to disclose incompatible third-party licenses.

## 5. Continuous development and standards

5.1 Development starts in the assigned project repository and remains continuously visible to authorized recipients. Initial, incomplete and intermediate work must be committed and pushed as part of the working process. A final release alone is not sufficient delivery. Internal company forks remain supplementary and subject to the same access restrictions.

5.2 Follow the repository's documented coding, architecture, interface, versioning, testing and documentation requirements. Include the editable source, README, relevant interface definitions, build and integration instructions and license notices needed to understand and use the contribution. A project standard is not inferred solely from an obsolete reference to a particular modeling or tracking product.

5.3 Use the actual task, issue, review and versioning systems designated for the repository. Maintain understandable links between work, development states and changes. Neither a mandatory historical Enterprise Architect/Pivotal installation nor a new numerical version format is imposed solely by this PJV.

5.4 Review and merge rights remain with the maintainers. The Coordinator may request correction or decline main-branch integration. Rights in an actual lawful submission arise under the LIC independently of merge. Accidental submission of another party's confidential or unlicensed material must be promptly reported and isolated.

5.5 Temporary connectivity or platform failure must be communicated. Keep lawful local work safe and synchronize it when the authorized channel is restored. Do not use outages as a reason to hide the development history. No fictional round-the-clock availability duty is created.

## 6. Cooperation and conduct

6.1 Members cooperate in good faith, communicate material blockers, respect other contributors, and support documented decisions and review. Harassment, impersonation, deliberate sabotage, unauthorized access and knowingly unlawful submissions are prohibited.

6.2 A voluntary Member is not required to supply an unspecified volume of future work or permanent availability. A separately agreed employment or paid service obligation remains governed by that contract. ADG membership alone creates no employment, wage, shareholding, profit share, reimbursement or future job entitlement.

6.3 Project funding, donations, crowdfunding and sponsorship must be undertaken by a person authorized for the relevant activity. Membership alone does not authorize collection of money in the project's or company's name. Separate financial arrangements must identify the responsible party and terms.

## 7. Contributions, rights and confidentiality

7.1 The LIC controls the contribution grant, continuing availability, necessary patent permissions, attribution, independent Product exclusion and authorized future license versions. This PJV adds no departure-based revocation, patent fee or automatic switch to Creative Commons or AGPL.

7.2 The Coordinator, other companies and individuals follow the same contribution rules. A Member must ensure the actual rightsholder has authorized the submission. Merely working during paid hours, using an AI tool or changing a copyright header does not settle ownership.

7.3 Restricted sources, credentials, nonpublic technical plans and other confidential materials may be used only within the authorized role. Do not publish them in public issues, AI services, repositories or logs without permission compatible with their confidentiality. Use third-party development services only where their terms and access are compatible with the materials entrusted to you.

7.4 The confidentiality obligation does not cover information lawfully public, independently developed without restricted information or lawfully received without restriction. Required disclosures are permitted to the legally necessary extent, with notice where lawful. It does not prevent lawful protected reporting.

7.5 Protect access credentials, report suspected misuse promptly and follow proportionate access-recovery instructions. On role change or departure, return or delete restricted materials except as permitted for surviving rights or mandatory records. Lawfully obtained public materials remain governed by their public license.

## 8. Membership status and departure

8.1 A Member may end voluntary participation by notice. Inform the responsible maintainer of outstanding work, push existing contribution states and identify handover information. Ending participation does not require creating new work beyond a separately existing lawful obligation.

8.2 A communicated absence may be recorded as inactive participation. No conflicting automatic three-month/six-month departure period from the historical documents applies to newly accepted membership under this version. Access may be limited to current role needs with notice; inactivity does not revoke already granted contribution rights.

8.3 For an identified remediable conduct or compliance issue, the Coordinator normally explains the issue and gives a reasonable opportunity to respond and remedy it. A serious security or rights risk may justify an immediate targeted access suspension. Permanent exclusion must identify its basis and respect applicable law.

8.4 Departure, exclusion, employment termination, a subscription change or death does not create a contractual right to withdraw validly granted contribution rights. Any mandatory statutory rights and lawful succession remain unaffected. A representative must establish authority before receiving restricted access or acting for a Member.

8.5 Re-admission requires a recorded decision and acceptance of the then-applicable terms. It is not automatic. Neither exclusion nor re-admission silently changes existing customer or employment contracts.

## 9. Disputes, privacy and responsibility

9.1 Members may request review of a technical or participation dispute by the responsible maintainer and, if unresolved, the designated project authority. Relevant persons must be heard proportionately. Internal review does not bar courts, urgent relief or statutory complaints.

9.2 Process membership records only for legitimate administration and lawful purposes. The Coordinator must supply appropriate privacy information, limit access and retention, and honor applicable data-protection duties. This PJV is not a blanket consent to unrelated data processing.

9.3 Responsibility for rights violations and applicable warranty/liability limits follows LIC sections 8 and 14. The advisory group or founder does not acquire blanket immunity, and a Member does not assume every other participant's liabilities.
